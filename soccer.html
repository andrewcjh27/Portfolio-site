01 — Why this section

A field where I keep
thinking out loud.

Soccer is the side of me that’s less polished and more obsessive — the intramural matches I play with my team, the formations I scribble on napkins, the goals I miss and replay in my head until practice.

This page collects the receipts: a running log of match scores I edit after every game, and a small AI coach I built to brainstorm starting lineups when half my team can’t make it.
