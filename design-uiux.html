UX

Product design, interface explorations, and user experience work — translating research and user needs into focused, useful screens.

Designing for
real people.

I treat UI/UX as the meeting point between research and craft — surveys, affinity mapping, and benchmarking on one side; layout, type, and component design on the other.

My favorite work happens when a design decision is traceable to an actual user behavior. The goal isn't to make something pretty in isolation, but to make the right thing easier for the person using it.

Featured Project

Braven Connect

A consulting engagement with Braven through the Berkeley Consulting Collaborative. I worked alongside a team of eight to synthesize multi-channel UX research — survey data, competitive benchmarking, and affinity mapping — into a focused design strategy for the mobile app.

We validated user needs through both qualitative and quantitative analysis, then iterated on the mobile interface to drive a measurable increase in engagement and usability.
