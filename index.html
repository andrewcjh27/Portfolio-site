about
me

I'm Junho, a student at UC Berkeley studying data science, integrative biology, and design. I'm drawn to the spaces where these fields overlap — using data to inform creative decisions and design to make the technical feel human. That curiosity shows up in my projects too, whether it's building a route optimizer that plans the shortest cafe tour across Seoul or training an AI to coach my recreational soccer team's formations in real time.

Outside of academics, I spend my time photographing the places I visit, rating every cafe I can find, and rethinking how my room should look. This site is a collection of all of it — the things I study, the tools I build, and the stuff I care about. If anything here catches your eye, feel free to reach out.
